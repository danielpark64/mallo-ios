SayRam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

SayRam ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information we collect

The app has no sign-up or login and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address or phone number.

The schedules you speak or type, your voice recordings and your alarm settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The developer runs no server and cannot access this data.

The app does not request internet access. It therefore cannot transmit your data off the device. It contains no ad network (ad SDK) and no analytics tools.

2. About speech recognition

Converting your speech to text is not done by the app itself. It is performed by the speech recognition service on your device (Android: the device's speech recognition service, e.g. Speech Services by Google; iOS: Apple speech recognition). Depending on your device settings and language, that service may process audio on the device or on the provider's (Google's or Apple's) servers, in which case the audio is handled under that provider's privacy policy. The app receives only the recognized text.

4. Backup, export and sharing with RotaWake

Data is copied elsewhere only when you run "Backup", "Export to folder" or "Export as text/PDF" yourself, and only to the location you choose (a device folder, another app, etc.). After that, storage and sharing follow the policies of the location you chose.

If the same developer's app RotaWake (교대알람) is installed on the same device and schedule sharing is turned on in either app (SayRam: Settings → "Share with RotaWake"), the two apps exchange each other's schedules (date, time and title) and shift roster only inside the device, without using the internet, and show them on each other's calendar. Recordings and spoken transcripts are never shared, and no other app can read this information. To stop sharing, turn it off in both apps. (Currently available on Android.)

5. Sharing with third parties

The app does not provide or sell your information to any third party.

6. Retention and deletion

Schedules and recordings are stored only on your device. Deleting a schedule in the app deletes its recording. Uninstalling the app deletes all data in the app (backups you exported separately are not affected).

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Promotional banner and external links

At the bottom of the schedule list there is a small promotional banner that shows the developer's YouTube channel, the developer's other app and a note about supporting the developer. The banner consists only of text and images bundled in the app. It uses no ad network and does not collect or transmit any user information. Whether RotaWake is already installed is checked only on the device; if it is, the banner does not show the RotaWake introduction. This result is never sent anywhere.

Tapping the banner opens your device's browser or the relevant app (YouTube, Google Play). From that point the privacy policy of that service applies. The support screen only displays account details; the app does not process any payment or transfer.
