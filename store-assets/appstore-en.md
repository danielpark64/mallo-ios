## Description (4,000) - Version

["Dentist tomorrow at 3 pm" - say it and you're done]

Tap the mic and speak the way you normally talk. SayRam picks out the date and time and writes it down as a schedule, then alerts you when the time comes. No more opening a calendar, picking a day, setting a time and typing a note.

Try "dinner with friends on Friday at 7 pm", "team meeting in 3 days at 10 am", "take my pills in 30 minutes" or "vacation from the 20th to the 24th".

[Made for you if]
- Typing schedules on a small keyboard feels like a chore
- Your hands are busy, driving or cooking, and you want to save a plan right now
- You'd rather hear what you actually said than read a short note
- You want a simple alarm and schedule app for your parents
- You want a light app you can use right away, with no sign-up

[Hear exactly what you said]
Every schedule keeps its recording. Tap the play button to hear your own voice, or open the detail screen and tap a word to replay just that part. Press and drag across words to hear only the section you need. Schedules without a recording can be heard too: tap play and your phone's voice reads out the date, time and content.

[Early reminders and an on-time alert]
Choose 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or a day before, or just say "remind me 30 minutes before". An alert arrives on time and again 1 and 2 minutes later in case you missed it. If your iPhone is in silent mode or a Focus is on, the alert may arrive without sound.

[A calendar you can read at a glance]
Like the iPhone Calendar, months scroll up and down and schedule names appear right on the days. Tap a day and its schedules slide up from the bottom. Pinch to zoom small text, or tap the year to see all 12 months on one screen.

[Vacations and business trips]
Say "vacation from the 20th to the 24th" and it is saved as a period, shown as a bar on the calendar. If you add something during your vacation, SayRam lets you know first.

[Plans that don't just disappear]
If you did it, leave a past schedule as it is. If you couldn't, only partly did, or the plan changed, tap it and choose under "How did it go?". The card gets a tag, and you can reschedule it to a new date with an alert. Upcoming plans can be postponed too ("Postpone"): the original date keeps a "Changed" tag with the new date.

[Schedule tab]
Upcoming schedules are grouped by day with easy labels like "tomorrow" or "in 3 days", and free days are shown in a single line. Search by content, pick a period and export it as text or PDF.

[No date? No problem]
If you don't say a date or time, like a quick "buy milk" or a whole meeting or lecture, it is saved at the time you recorded it. Tap "Set" later to choose a date and get an alert.

[Backup and moving to a new phone]
Back up all schedules and recordings to one folder, then pick that folder on your new phone to bring everything back.

[No login, no server, no ad tracking]
There is no account and no login. Schedules and recordings stay only on this device, and the app sends your data nowhere. (Turning speech into text is handled by Apple speech recognition on your device.) A small banner at the bottom of the list points to the developer's YouTube channel. No ad network is used.

[Light]
SayRam only works while you record or play back. Nothing keeps running in the background.

[Dark and light themes, 4 text sizes, 9 languages]
Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

Say it, and it's scheduled. SayRam.
