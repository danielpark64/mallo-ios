<en-US>
The first release of SayRam.
• Say it — SayRam hears the date and time and sets the schedule and alarm
• Replay your own voice, or tap a word to hear just that part
• Early reminders: 10 min, 30 min, 1 hour or a day before
• Scrolling calendar and multi-day schedules like vacations
• Mark past schedules as not done, partly done or changed; postpone upcoming ones
• Search, text & PDF export, backup
