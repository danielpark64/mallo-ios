## Full description

🎙 "Dentist tomorrow at 3pm" — say it and you're done

Tap the mic and speak the way you normally talk. SayRam picks out the date and time, saves it as a schedule, and rings an alarm when the time comes. No more opening a calendar, picking a day, setting a time and typing a note.

Try "dinner with friends next Friday at 7", "team meeting in 3 days at 10am", or "take my pills in 30 minutes".

📌 Made for you if
• Typing schedules on a small keyboard feels like a chore
• Your hands are busy — driving, cooking — and you need to save a plan right now
• You'd rather hear what you actually said than read a short note
• You want a simple alarm and schedule app for your parents
• You want a light app you can use right away, with no sign-up

🔁 Hear exactly what you said
Every schedule keeps its recording. Tap ▶ on a card to hear your own voice, or open the detail screen and tap a single word to replay just that part. Long-press and drag across words to loop the section you need.
(Saved recordings and word replay need Android 13 or later. On Android 12 and below your words are saved as text, and schedules and alarms work the same.)

⏰ Early reminders + an on-time alarm
An alarm at the exact appointment time is already too late. Choose any of 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or a day before — or just say "remind me 30 minutes before". At the set time the alarm rings, and rings again 1 and 2 minutes later in case you missed it. Pick sound + vibration, sound only, or vibration only for each schedule.

📅 Calendar and list in one view
Month or week calendar on top, that period's schedules listed by day below. Past items are folded away so what's coming up stays on top. Day-before reminders show up on the day they ring.

🤔 Forgot to say when? Nothing is lost
Say "buy milk" with no date and the recording is still kept, tagged "Date not set yet". Set the day whenever you're ready.

🔎 Search and export
Search by content, or filter by today, this week, this month or any period. Preview the result as text or PDF, then export it.

💾 Backup and move to a new phone
Back up all schedules and recordings to one folder, then pick that folder on your new phone to bring everything back.

🚫 No sign-up · No internet permission · No ad tracking
There is no account and no login. Your schedules and recordings stay on your phone. The app does not even request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere. (Turning speech into text is handled by the speech recognition service on your phone.) There is one small banner at the bottom of the list that points to the developer's channel and other app. No ad network is used, so nothing is collected for advertising.

🪶 Light on your phone
SayRam only works while you record or play back. Nothing keeps running in the background.

🌗 Dark / light theme · 4 text sizes
Choose the theme that's easy on your eyes and one of four text sizes. Larger text also switches the layout so nothing gets cramped.

🌏 9 languages
한국어, English, 日本語, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português. Change the app language and the spoken language follows.

Say it, and it's scheduled — SayRam.
